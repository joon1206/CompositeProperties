Primary question: **For the illustrative balanced 0/90 glass-fiber laminate
with silica-filled resin, which assumptions in the supplied hierarchical
calculation dominate changes in predicted nine elastic constants and three
CTEs: the final laminate averaging, its thermal eigenstrain equations, or the
filled-matrix closure?**

fixed over fiber/silica fractions? This was selected *after* the pilot sweep
