# Open scientific questions

- What are the measured resin modulus, Poisson ratio and CTE over the intended
  operating temperature range, with cure state and moisture recorded?
- Is the 70 vol% silica fraction within the *non-fiber* matrix achieved with
  a particle-size distribution, ordering, or another architecture? What are
  its porosity and interfacial properties?
- Does the actual reinforcement consist of ideal unidirectional 0/90 plies
  or woven fabric? If woven, the present ply model is not directly applicable.
- Can paired directional mechanical and dilatometric data on the same
  laminate discriminate the two filled-matrix closures?
