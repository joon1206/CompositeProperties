# Research decision

The selected study is a numerical audit with two linked material closures and
an exact flat-layer reference. It was chosen because the formulas and
reference boundary conditions can be checked rigorously and the inputs are
explicit. Direct hierarchical fiber/silica RVEs already exist in the
[2017 polyimide study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compositesa.2017.02.015),
and exact layered thermoelasticity already exists in
[Chen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0266-3538(02)00017-9). We reject any
claim that their combination is new.

The methods rank *model-form sensitivity*, not predictive accuracy against
the user's actual material. A full particle/fiber RVE would introduce
unidentified particle size distributions, interfaces, packing, and porosity;
it cannot validate the illustrative inputs. This is why a 3D RVE was not
used as a decorative third level. The independent constrained-energy
flat-layer calculation is the numerical reference for the selected question.
