# Research gaps after four novelty passes

The exact 3D thermoelastic layered method, silica-filled glass-fiber
micromechanics, and laminate CTE optimization are established. A numerical
comparison of the pasted formulas is valuable as a case-specific audit, but
is not yet a publication-level novelty claim.

An experimentally defensible gap remains: measure the *same* glass/resin/
silica formulation and laminate, including particle size distribution,
porosity, cure, temperature, and all three directional CTEs and moduli. Then
test whether matrix closure or laminate averaging dominates prediction error.
The present project has no such dataset, so that physical hypothesis remains
unvalidated. Acquiring these measurements is outside this computational run.
